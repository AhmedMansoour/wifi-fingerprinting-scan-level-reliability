!!! warning "The archive provides aggregated results, not 80 individual accuracy cells"
    The approved estimator summary reports results **aggregated across ten units** for eight estimators. This page intentionally does not depict a measured 10 × 8 unit–estimator heatmap: the complete cell-level error table is not available in the released approved aggregates. Indicator results are summaries across up to 80 dataset–estimator conditions, with fewer valid conditions for some metrics. No missing cell is inferred or simulated.

**Data provenance:** the visualization is generated from `results/approved/legacy_canonical/estimator_error_summary.csv` and the three revised A3 association/discrimination summaries. See [approved research assets](../EXISTING_RESEARCH_ASSETS.md) and [what results establish](evidence.md).

The displayed statistics are for retrospective evaluation. SQS is not a calibrated probability of position correctness.
